# Character Creation

[← Player Campaign Guide](./)

Characters begin at **5th level** and use the **2014 D&D 5th Edition rules baseline**.

**Official 2014-era D&D 5e material appropriate to the Forgotten Realms is allowed.** Setting-specific material from other settings—such as *Dragonlance* or *Spelljammer*—is not part of the normal character-creation pool.

## Approved Character Sources

- *Player's Handbook* (2014)
- *Sword Coast Adventurer's Guide*
- *Elemental Evil Player's Companion*
- *Volo's Guide to Monsters*
- *Xanathar's Guide to Everything*
- *Mordenkainen's Tome of Foes*
- *Tasha's Cauldron of Everything*
- *Mordenkainen Presents: Monsters of the Multiverse*
- *Waterdeep: Dragon Heist* — player character options

*Baldur's Gate: Descent into Avernus* is a campaign/adventure source, but it is **not** part of the normal player character-creation source list.

If you are unsure whether a D&D Beyond option comes from an approved source, ask the DM.

## Feats and Multiclassing

- **Feats are allowed.**
- **Multiclassing is allowed.**

## Backgrounds

Players may customize backgrounds using the **2014 Player's Handbook background-customization rules**. Published backgrounds can also be used as written.

No current pre-Session-1 character is assumed to have customized a background unless that character's player has actually done so.

## Ability Scores

Use **point buy** for ability scores.

## Starting Equipment

Because characters begin at 5th level, use the campaign's higher-level starting equipment package:

- normal class and background starting equipment;
- **500 gp + 1d10 × 25 gp**;
- **one uncommon magic item of the player's choice**.

The chosen uncommon magic item should be compatible with the campaign's allowed 2014-era Forgotten Realms material.

## Hit Points and Encumbrance

- **Do not roll for hit points when gaining levels.** Use the fixed hit-point increase for the character's class instead.
- **Encumbrance matters.** The campaign uses the 2014 **Variant: Encumbrance** rules. Track what the character is carrying and the applicable encumbrance thresholds.

## Start With the Character

Start with the character you want to play rather than trying to reverse-engineer a character for the campaign plot.

Think about:

- who the character is;
- why they are in or around Waterdeep;
- what matters to them;
- what might make them willing to help when something dangerous happens;
- whether they already know someone involved or would rather meet everyone during play.

A detailed backstory is welcome if you enjoy writing one, but it is not required. A few strong ideas are enough.

## Player Authority

Your character's important personal choices belong to you. This includes things such as class, subclass, faith, background, personality, relationships, motivations, and personal history.

The DM may offer possibilities or campaign connections, but those connections should respond to the character you create rather than secretly deciding your character's history for you.

## Connecting to the Campaign

You do not need a connection to the previous *Dragon Heist* party.

Possible starting relationships include:

- knowing someone already involved;
- having an independent reason to become involved;
- being a stranger who is drawn into events.

The best option depends on the character.

## 2014 Rules

Be careful when using D&D Beyond. Newer 2024 mechanics and terminology can appear alongside legacy character material.

The campaign uses the 2014 rules unless a later source or campaign decision is intentionally being used. If something on a character sheet looks unusual, ask the DM before changing the build.

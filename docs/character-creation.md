# Character Creation

[← Player Campaign Guide](./)

Characters begin at **5th level** and use the **2014 D&D 5th Edition rules baseline**.

## Start With the Character

Start with the character you want to play rather than trying to reverse-engineer a character for the campaign plot.

Think about:

- who the character is;
- why they are in or around Waterdeep;
- what matters to them;
- what might make them willing to help when something dangerous happens;
- whether they already know someone involved or would rather meet everyone during play.

A detailed backstory is welcome if you enjoy writing one, but it is not required. A few strong ideas are enough.

## Player Authority

Your character's important personal choices belong to you. This includes things such as class, subclass, faith, background, personality, relationships, motivations, and personal history.

The DM may offer possibilities or campaign connections, but those connections should respond to the character you create rather than secretly deciding your character's history for you.

## Connecting to the Campaign

You do not need a connection to the previous *Dragon Heist* party.

Possible starting relationships include:

- knowing someone already involved;
- having an independent reason to become involved;
- being a stranger who is drawn into events.

The best option depends on the character.

## 2014 Rules

Be careful when using D&D Beyond. Newer 2024 mechanics and terminology can appear alongside legacy character material.

The campaign uses the 2014 rules unless a later source or campaign decision is intentionally being used. If something on a character sheet looks unusual, ask the DM before changing the build.

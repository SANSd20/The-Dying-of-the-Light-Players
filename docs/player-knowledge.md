# Player Knowledge

[← Player Campaign Guide](./)

This site is a **shared player reference**. Information published here is safe for everyone at the table to read.

That does not mean every player character automatically knows everything on the site.

## Player Knowledge

Players may use this guide to understand the campaign, table rules, prior-campaign context, and other information the DM has intentionally made available to the whole table.

For example, the players know that **Avernus, the first layer of the Nine Hells, is an intended major destination** for the campaign.

## Character Knowledge

A character knows only what makes sense from that character's history and what they learn during play.

At the beginning of the campaign, a character does not automatically know:

- that the group will eventually travel to Avernus;
- everything that happened during the previous *Dragon Heist* campaign;
- another PC's private history or motivations;
- DM-only explanations for mysteries the party has not solved.

## Character Privacy

Player-specific motivations, secrets, private relationships, and unrevealed backstory are not automatically published here.

They can be added after the player reveals them in play or approves them for the shared guide.

## Discoveries During Play

As the campaign progresses, information the table discovers can be added to this site. The player guide should record what has become safe for the whole table without exposing future developments or DM-only explanations.

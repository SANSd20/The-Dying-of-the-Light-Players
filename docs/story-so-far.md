# The Story So Far

[← Player Campaign Guide](./)

You do **not** need to know *Waterdeep: Dragon Heist* to join this campaign.

A previous adventuring company became involved in the struggle over Waterdeep's missing hoard of dragons. One of those adventurers was **Ida**, a human Light Domain cleric of Lathander who had come to Waterdeep on a pilgrimage.

The company eventually became involved with the prominent Cassalanter family.

On the day of a Cassalanter event, Ida was away completing the sacred rite that had originally brought her to Waterdeep. The other members of the company attended without her.

They did not return.

The other adventurers died during the events surrounding the Cassalanters, and their bodies were burned. Ida survived because she was elsewhere completing her rite.

At approximately the same time, Ida completed her pilgrimage and received the religious title **Dawnbringer**, recognizing spiritual growth and readiness for greater independent service to Lathander.

Only afterward did Ida receive the complete message her companions had tried to get to her:

> **You are in danger.**

There was no additional explanation.

Ida has begun trying to discover what happened.

## What This Means for a New Character

Your character does not automatically know all of the history above. This page gives **you, the player**, enough background to understand the campaign you are joining.

What your character knows will depend on who they are and how they enter the story.

Devils and infernal forces will play an important role in the campaign, but how they connect to these events is part of the mystery.

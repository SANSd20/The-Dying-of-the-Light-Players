# The Dying of the Light

## Player Campaign Guide

**Dungeons & Dragons 5th Edition — 2014 Rules**

Welcome to *The Dying of the Light*. This campaign continues from the aftermath of a completed *Waterdeep: Dragon Heist* campaign, but **you do not need to have played the previous campaign** to join.

The campaign begins at **5th level** and uses **milestone advancement**.

> **Pre-Session 1:** This player guide is currently being prepared. More campaign background, character-creation information, table expectations, and spoiler-safe setting material will be added before play begins.

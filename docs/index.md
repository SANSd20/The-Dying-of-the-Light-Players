# The Dying of the Light

## Player Campaign Guide

**Dungeons & Dragons 5th Edition — 2014 Rules**

Welcome to *The Dying of the Light*. This campaign continues from the aftermath of a completed *Waterdeep: Dragon Heist* campaign. What begins in Waterdeep as an attempt to understand a tragedy will uncover a larger mystery involving devils and infernal forces.

The campaign begins at **5th level** and uses **milestone advancement**.

## Site Purpose

This site is the table's **shared, player-safe campaign reference**. Information here is safe for every player to read, but it is not automatically knowledge every player character possesses.

DM-only secrets, future developments, encounter preparation, and unrevealed character information are kept elsewhere.

## Campaign Basics

- **Starting level:** 5
- **Rules:** D&D 5th Edition — 2014 rules
- **Advancement:** Milestone
- **Starting location:** Waterdeep
- **Campaign year:** 1492 DR
- **Major destination:** Avernus, the first layer of the Nine Hells

### D&D Beyond

This campaign uses the **2014 rules**. D&D Beyond may display newer 2024 material alongside 2014 material. Make sure character options use the campaign's 2014 baseline, and ask if something is uncertain.

## The Story So Far

A previous company of adventurers became involved in the struggle over Waterdeep's missing hoard of dragons. Among them was **Ida**, a human cleric of Lathander who had come to Waterdeep on a pilgrimage.

Eventually the company became involved with the prominent Cassalanter family.

On the day of a Cassalanter event, Ida was away completing the sacred rite that had originally brought her to Waterdeep. The other members of the company attended without her.

They did not return.

The other adventurers died during the events surrounding the Cassalanters, and their bodies were burned. Ida survived because she was elsewhere completing her rite.

At approximately the same time, Ida completed her pilgrimage and received the religious title **Dawnbringer**, recognizing spiritual growth and readiness for greater independent service to Lathander.

Only afterward did she receive a message her companions had tried to get to her:

> **You are in danger.**

There was no explanation. Ida has begun trying to discover what happened.

That is where *The Dying of the Light* begins.

## Creating Your Character

Start with the character you actually want to play.

Consider who they are, why they are in or around Waterdeep, what matters to them, and what might make them willing to help when something dangerous happens.

You do not need an elaborate backstory. A few strong ideas are enough, and additional history can emerge during play.

Character choices such as class, subclass, faith, background, relationships, motivations, personality, and personal history belong to the player. Campaign connections will be built around those choices rather than imposed on the character.

## Tone and Themes

*The Dying of the Light* is heroic fantasy that can move into darker territory. The campaign is expected to take the party to **Avernus, the first layer of the Nine Hells**. The characters do not necessarily know that at the beginning; how and why the journey occurs is part of the story.

Themes include **life, hope, dawn, renewal, loss, difficult choices, and refusing to surrender something merely because saving it is hard**.

The campaign may include death, devils, infernal themes, supernatural horror, disturbing imagery, and morally difficult situations. It is **not intended to be unrelenting grimdark**. Humor, friendship, strange situations, victories, and genuine hope remain part of the game.

If there is material you do not want included at the table, tell the DM. You can do so privately.

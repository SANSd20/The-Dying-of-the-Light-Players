# Player Characters

[← Player Campaign Guide](./)

**Pre-Session 1:** These are characters expected to be involved when the campaign begins. Except for Ida's established history from the previous campaign, this does not mean these characters have already met or formed a party.

## Ida

**Human Cleric 5 — Light Domain — Acolyte**

Ida is a follower of Lathander and the sole surviving member of the previous adventuring company. She came to Waterdeep on a pilgrimage. Completing her sacred rite granted her the religious title **Dawnbringer**.

Ida was away performing that rite when her companions died. Afterward she received their complete message:

> **You are in danger.**

She has begun investigating what happened.

## Pip

**Lupin Warlock 5 — Archfey — Pact of the Chain — Charlatan**

Pip is a pre-campaign character being prepared for *The Dying of the Light*.

Pip's conversion to the campaign's 2014 rules baseline is still being finalized. Personal motivations, relationships, and other player-specific information are intentionally not included in this shared guide.

## Ko'Maru

**Tortle Paladin 5 — Oath of the Ancients — Soldier**

Ko'Maru is a quiet, protective follower of Eldath who prefers peaceful solutions but will fight to defend others.

Ko'Maru uses the Tortle mechanics from *Mordenkainen Presents: Monsters of the Multiverse*. Personal motivations, relationships, and unrevealed backstory are intentionally omitted from this shared guide.

## Gilly

**Tiefling Ranger 5 — Beast Master — Farmer**

Gilly is a pre-campaign character. Personal history, motivations, relationships, and unrevealed backstory are intentionally omitted from this shared guide.

Some current sheet entries may come from D&D Beyond's 2024 rules presentation and are still under review against the campaign's 2014 baseline.

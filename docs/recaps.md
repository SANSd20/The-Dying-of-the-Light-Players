# Campaign Recaps

[← Player Campaign Guide](./)

This page will hold **player-safe records of what actually happens during play**.

## Pre-Session 1

The campaign has not yet begun.

Once sessions are played, recaps can be added here. Played events take precedence over preparation or expected outcomes.

Recaps should record what happened at the table without exposing DM-only information that the players have not discovered.

# The Dying of the Light — Player Guide

This repository contains the **player-facing campaign guide** for *The Dying of the Light*, a Dungeons & Dragons 5th Edition campaign.

The guide is intentionally spoiler-safe. DM-only campaign development, hidden history, encounter preparation, and future plot material belong in the separate campaign repository and should not be copied here.

## Status

**Pre-Session 1**

The player guide is being developed as the table's ongoing, spoiler-safe campaign reference.

## Rules baseline

- Dungeons & Dragons 5th Edition — **2014 rules**
- Starting level: **5**
- Advancement: **milestone**

## About the campaign

*The Dying of the Light* continues from a completed *Waterdeep: Dragon Heist* campaign and is expected to take the party to **Avernus, the first layer of the Nine Hells**.

This repository is for information safe for the entire player table. DM-only history, secrets, future developments, and encounter preparation remain in the separate campaign repository.
